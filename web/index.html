Math Toys

Small pieces of maths you can play with. Draw something, poke at it, and watch the ideas move. Each toy explains itself step by step as you scroll down, with a few fun facts along the way.

Circles

Draw any closed shape and get its equation. A chain of spinning circles draws it back, and more circles make it sharper. This is the Fourier series.

Draw a shape

Distance, speed, acceleration

Draw how far something has gone, how fast it moves, or how hard it speeds up, and see the other two. Slopes go one way, areas go the other.
