# The shape of a concept page

Every concept on Lilavati is one page, named after the **concept** (Fourier series, Derivatives and
integrals), never after the example used to show it (circles, distance and velocity). The example is
how we teach it; the concept is what the reader learns and what they would search for.

Each page has the same sections, in the same order, with the same ids. `web/concepts.js` reads them
to build the "On this page" bar, so a section only shows up there if it follows this table.

| # | Section | id | What goes in it | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hero** | `hero` | Concept name (h1), the big idea in one sentence (`.idea`), two or three sentences of what you can do here. Topic and level chips come from `concepts.js`. | 3 sentences |
| 2 | **Play** | `play` | The interactive toy: the thing to poke at. Starts already showing something alive (a preset), never a blank canvas. | — |
| 3 | **Try this** | `try` | Three to five small experiments, each "do X, notice Y". Each one makes one point of the concept visible. Buttons may set the toy up. | 3–5 items |
| 4 | **Step by step** | `steps` | Four to seven numbered steps from the reader's own input to the idea. Each step: a short heading that states the point, two short paragraphs, and a figure drawn **from what the reader set up above**, so the walk-through follows the reader. **Each figure derives its result visually**: show the intermediate quantities and how they combine into the number or formula (animate the process where it helps), never only the final answer. | 4–7 steps |
| 5 | **In symbols** | `symbols` | The formulas, each with a one-line reading in plain words underneath. Only notation the steps already explained. | 2–4 formulas |
| 6 | **Where you meet it** | `uses` | Real places the concept is at work: things the reader already uses or has felt. | 3–6 items |
| 7 | **Fun facts** | `facts` | History, records and surprises. Each one true and checkable, with a name, a date or a number. | 4–6 cards |
| 8 | **Common mix-ups** | `mixups` | Mistakes people really make: "It's not X; it's Y" with one line of why. | 2–4 items |
| 9 | **Read more** (optional) | `read` | Sources to go deeper: lecture notes, books, papers, each with one line on what it adds. Check every link opens. | 3–5 links |
| 10 | **Next** | `next` | Related concepts. Generated by `concepts.js` from the `related` list; do not write by hand. | — |

## Chapters

A concept can belong to a chapter (a `series` in `web/concepts.js`, for example *Linear algebra for AI*). Its pages then
show "Part n of m", link to the previous and next part, and appear together on the home page. The linear
algebra pages share `web/la.js`: a coordinate plane, draggable arrows, a matrix editor and the presets.

## Markup

```html
<body data-concept="fourier-series">
  <header id="hero" class="hero"> <h1>…</h1> <p class="idea">…</p> <p>…</p> </header>
  <section id="play" data-label="Play"> … the toy … </section>
  <section id="try" data-label="Try this" class="block reveal"> <h2>Try this</h2> <ol class="try"> <li><strong>Do</strong> … <span>Notice …</span></li> </ol> </section>
  <section id="steps" data-label="Step by step" class="block"> <h2>…</h2> <div class="step reveal"> <div><h3><span class="num">1</span>…</h3> <p>…</p></div> <figure>…</figure> </div> </section>
  <section id="symbols" data-label="In symbols" class="block reveal"> <div class="symbols"> <div><code class="formula">…</code><p>…</p></div> </div> </section>
  <section id="uses" data-label="Where you meet it" class="block reveal"> <ul class="uses"> <li><strong>…</strong> …</li> </ul> </section>
  <section id="facts" data-label="Fun facts" class="block reveal"> <div class="facts"> <article><h4>…</h4><p>…</p></article> </div> </section>
  <section id="mixups" data-label="Common mix-ups" class="block reveal"> <ul class="mixups"> <li><strong>Not:</strong> … <strong>But:</strong> …</li> </ul> </section>
  <section id="next" data-label="Next" class="block"></section>
  <script type="module" src="../concepts.js"></script>
</body>
```

## Adding a concept

1. Add it to `CONCEPTS` in `web/concepts.js` (slug, name, idea, topic, level, colour, related). With
   `status: 'soon'` it shows on the home page as coming next.
2. Maths in `src/<module>.rs` with tests; pages import `../pkg/lilavati.js`.
3. Copy `docs/concept-template.html` to `web/<slug>/index.html` and fill in every section.
4. Set `status: 'live'`, and add a small `preview(canvas, t)` drawing for the home page card.

## Voice

Plain words, short sentences, the reader as "you". Name the thing before the symbol. Numbers with units.
